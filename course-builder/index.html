Course Builder

Plan your week. Send it to HomeSchool Scribe.

Give each child a color lane, put courses on days, type in how many lessons or pages a book has and the lessons fill themselves in. Then download one file and open it in the app. Nothing you already have in the app changes.

Who's learning?

Each child gets a color lane. Pick a color and a grade.

Which school year is this for?

The year names the file and helps the app pick the right year. No dates go in the file.

Your week

Add a course in a lane, then drag it onto days or tap a shortcut. Tap a block to open it.

Lessons

Each course needs its lessons. Add them here, or leave a course empty and plan it as you go in the app.

Preview

A day the way the app's Today screen lays it out. The app uses your own school days and away days.

Review your plan

Your family's week, each child's day, every course through its last lesson, and the file that carries it all.

Now open it in the app

  • Get it to your iPhone or iPad: AirDrop Our week from your Downloads folder, or save it to iCloud Drive, or text or email it to yourself.
  • Then tap the file in Files, Messages or Mail, and HomeSchool Scribe shows what it will add before it adds anything.
  • Or from inside the app: Courses, tap +, then From a Course Builder file... and pick the file.
  • Building this on the phone itself? Tap Safari's download arrow, tap the file, then Share, then HomeSchool Scribe.

What the app will do

The app shows what it will add before it adds anything. Nothing already in HomeSchool Scribe is changed or removed.

Before you download

    Share link

    The link carries the shape of your week only: the days, the colors, the course names and how often they meet. No names, no lesson text, no notes. It lives inside the link itself; nothing is sent to us.

    Make a co-op pack

    A file holding only the co-op's courses. Each family opens it in HomeSchool Scribe and picks which child takes each color. Nothing else travels: no names, no other courses, nothing ongoing.

    You have a week in progress

    It was saved in this browser only. The names you typed for each child were not saved; you can type them again.
