What HomeSchool Scribe is

HomeSchool Scribe is a planner and record-keeper for families who teach at home. You enter your school's details, students, courses, and lessons; the app keeps attendance, hours, grades, portfolios, transcripts, and other records from what you check off, and prepares documents you may choose to file with your state.

It is not legal advice

The app includes summaries of homeschool requirements for each U.S. state and the District of Columbia, deadline reminders, and documents prepared in a state's format. These are planning aids written in plain language. They are not legal advice, they may be out of date, and they are not a substitute for reading your state's law or asking a homeschool legal organization. You are responsible for confirming what your state requires and for filing it. The app never submits anything to a state on your behalf; when it opens a state website, you are dealing with that website, not with us.

Your account, your data

You can export them at any time as a backup file from Records ▸ Backup, and you can delete them by deleting records in the app, deleting the app, or clearing the app's iCloud data in iOS Settings. Our privacy policy explains this in more detail.
