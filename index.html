How it works

Plan once. Check off. Print when they ask.

HomeSchool Scribe turns the thing you already do every morning, checking off lessons, into the records your state, an evaluator, or a college will ask for later.

Plan a term in one tap

Add a course, choose the days, and HomeSchool Scribe lays out the lessons around your breaks. Paste a table of contents and it becomes a term.

Records that fill themselves in

Check a lesson and attendance is marked, hours are logged, and grades update. Every child on one screen; kids get their own list.

Paperwork, ready

Attendance reports, report cards, transcripts with GPA, portfolios, and your state's filings, as PDFs, with reminders before they're due.

Records

Built from what you already checked off.

Every document a homeschool gets asked for, drawn from the same check marks. Print any of them the day someone asks.

  • Attendance and hours. Days, hours, monthly totals, and a signed certification page.
  • Report cards and transcripts. Weighted and unweighted GPA, credits, course of study, test scores.
  • Portfolio. Photos of work filed by student, course, and date, two to a page.
  • State filings. Notice letters, worksheets, and progress reports in your state's format.
  • A backup you own. Export everything as one JSON file at any time.

Student Mode

Kids get their own list.

Hand over the iPad and each child sees only their day: big checkboxes, a timer for the lesson they're on, and a button that tells you when they're stuck. A parent PIN keeps the rest of the app closed.

Their check marks become your attendance and hours, and the timer feeds the hours log, so the record is kept without anyone keeping it.

Privacy

Your family's records stay in your iCloud.

HomeSchool Scribe has no accounts and no servers. Your records sync between your devices through your own iCloud account, and the app collects nothing about you: no analytics, no ads, no third-party code. Student Mode collects nothing about your children.

Captions and progress summaries are written by Apple's on-device model on your iPhone; nothing is sent anywhere.

Made by a homeschooler

I was homeschooled. Every summer, before the school year started, my mom spent weeks at the kitchen table writing out the whole year by hand, one child at a time: a folder for each of us, a page for each subject, every assignment with its page numbers and the problems to do. Each morning I opened my folder and checked things off. She never complained, but I saw the weeks it took, and I saw her do it again the next summer.

HomeSchool Scribe is that folder, and those weeks, in your pocket. Add a course, pick the days, and the year lays itself out. Check off the day and the records write themselves, so the weeks she spent on paper can go back to your kids. It's built in Villa Rica, Georgia, by a homeschool graduate who wanted every homeschool mom to have it a little easier than mine did. Every support email gets read by the person who wrote the app.
