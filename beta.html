HomeSchool Scribe · TestFlight

Founding families

Before HomeSchool Scribe reaches the App Store this January, fifty families will run their real school days on it for about three weeks and tell us everything. There are fifty places, and they go to the first fifty eligible joins through our TestFlight link, which we email to the list below starting October 1. Founding families who take part receive their first year of Scribe Plus free at public launch.

Sign up for the beta

We'll email you the TestFlight link when the beta opens on October 1, then a short note each week. Nothing else, and you can leave any time.

The fifty places are taken by the first fifty eligible joins through that link; once they are gone, the link stops accepting new testers. Signing up here holds your place in the email, not in the beta.

iPhone and iPad only (iOS 26). There is no Android or web version, so please sign up with the Apple device you'll use.

Who we're looking for

  • An iPhone or iPad on iOS 26 (Settings ▸ General ▸ About shows the version).
  • Any number of students, any grades; we especially want a few high-schoolers for the transcript.
  • A mix of states. Georgia, New York, Pennsylvania, Ohio, Virginia, North Carolina, and Florida have paperwork we want to get right; Texas and other easy states matter too.
  • About three weeks of real use, counted from the day you actually get access: your real students, your real curriculum, checked off on real school days.

What you'll do

  1. Install TestFlight, Apple's free app for trying apps before release, from the App Store.
  2. Open our link from the email we send you and tap Install. Updates arrive through TestFlight about once a week; it will tell you.
  3. Set up your school the way you'd really run it: your state, your school year, your students, and their courses. You are joining mid-year, so on the year screen leave "Mark the school days since… present" on: the weeks you already did count, and lessons plan from today. If Records shows something that was due before you started, answer it once: Sent before I started, or Dismiss. Then plan a week and check things off each day.
  4. Try what your state asks for. Open Records, look at the deadlines it lists for your state, and generate the attendance report, a report card, and, for a high-schooler, the transcript. Read them as if you were handing them to an evaluator.
  5. Hand a child Student Mode for a day and see what they make of it.
  6. Tell us everything. Confusing, wrong, slow, or just "I expected this somewhere else" all count. In TestFlight, take a screenshot and it offers to send it to us with your device details; or email support@myhomeschoolscribe.com. Please cover your children's names in screenshots.

What a week looks like

Three weeks sounds like a lot to ask. In practice it is a few minutes a day, and most of it is the checking off you already do on paper.

Day oneSet your school up the way you really run it: your state, your school days, your children and their subjects. Twenty minutes, once. If you started school before you installed this, leave the "Mark the school days since… present" switch on so the weeks you already did are counted.
Every school dayOpen Today and check off what you did. A minute or two. Attendance is marked, hours are logged and grades update as you go. If a subject has no plan, write a line under it — "Frog and Toad, ch. 3" — and that line is the record.
Once in week onePlan a week the way you would on paper: add a course, choose the days, and let it lay the lessons out. Photograph a book's contents page if you would rather not type.
Week twoPrint what your state or an evaluator would ask for: Records ▸ Attendance and hours, a report card, and for a high-schooler the transcript. Read them as if you were handing them over.
Week threeTell us what was confusing, wrong, slow, or missing. We will also ask, once, whether you would share a sentence for the website. Saying no changes nothing.

How to tell us something

  • From TestFlight, with a screenshot. Take a screenshot anywhere in the app; TestFlight offers to send it to us with your device and version details attached. That is the most useful kind of report, because we can see exactly what you saw.
  • Or email support@myhomeschoolscribe.com. Tell us what you expected and what happened; neither has to be tidy.
  • Please cover your children's names before you send a screenshot, or use initials. We do not need them, and we would rather not have them.
  • "This is obvious" reports are the valuable ones. If you could not find something, that is our fault, not yours, and it is exactly what we need to hear.

What to expect

Is my family's data safe in a beta?

Yes. The app has no accounts and no servers; your records stay on your device and in your own iCloud. Beta builds are the same app, a few weeks early. Export a backup from Records ▸ Backup whenever you like.

What if something breaks?

Tell us and we fix it, usually in the next weekly build. Your records aren't affected by app updates.

Does it cost anything?

No. Scribe Plus is unlocked for the beta. Founding families who take part receive their first year of Scribe Plus free at public launch. We send the code at launch.

Will you use my name?

Only if you say so. In week three we'll ask whether you'd share a sentence or two for the website with your first name and state. Saying no changes nothing.

When does the beta start?

October 1, 2026. The build has already cleared Apple's beta review, so the link works the moment we send it. Your three weeks run from the day you install, not from a fixed date, so a family that joins a week late simply finishes a week later. The App Store release is planned for January 5, 2027.
