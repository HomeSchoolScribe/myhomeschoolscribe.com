Homeschool requirements · Wyoming

Homeschooling in Wyoming

Light paperwork · A notice or an enrollment form, then keep your own records.

Submit your curriculum to the local school board each year showing a basic academic program.

Deadlines

Wyoming lets a family homeschool more than one way, and the paperwork differs by route. You pick the route once in the app and it tracks only those dates. Districts vary; the app lets you edit any of them.

Home-based educational program

The ordinary route, defined in W.S. 21-4-101(a)(v) and 21-4-102(b). The curriculum covers reading, writing, mathematics, civics, history, literature and science. Since July 1, 2025 nothing goes to the school district: no notice, no curriculum submission, no testing, no reports.

Course of study for the year
Records · Keep in your files		When you start the school yearKeep in your filesW.S. 21-4-102(b) leaves one continuing duty on the person running the program: ensure a curriculum is administered covering reading, writing, mathematics, civics, history, literature and science, sequentially progressive. Nobody signs off on it. House Bill 46 (2025 Enrolled Act 28) removed the annual curriculum submission to the local board of trustees effective July 1, 2025, so there is no filing and no deadline attached to this. Writing the year's course of study down at the start is simply the easiest way to show later what the program covered.
Withdrawal from public school
Notice · Bring to a meeting		Once, when you beginThe school district the child is leavingThis one is about leaving a Wyoming public school, and it only reaches a child under 18. W.S. 21-4-102(c) describes an in-person meeting with a district counselor or administrator and written consent to the withdrawal. The statute applies where the parent has not otherwise notified the district that the child is enrolling in another district, a private school, or a home-based educational program, so notifying the district first is what the statute treats as replacing the meeting. The consent form carries a separate provision releasing the student's name and address to the Wyoming National Guard Youth Challenge program under W.S. 19-9-701. Districts handle the paperwork differently, so the district office is the place to ask what it wants. Filed once when a child leaves public school; it does not repeat each year, and a child who was never enrolled in a Wyoming public school has nothing here to do.
Nothing to file: keep your own records
Records · Keep in your files		By the end of the school yearKeep in your filesA Wyoming home-based educational program files nothing and is not registered anywhere, so the family's own file is the only account of it. Attendance and the course of study answer most questions. One point the popular summaries blur: the 175-day school year in W.S. 21-4-301(b) is a rule for school districts, not for a home-based educational program, and the statutes set no day count, hour count, testing requirement or record-retention period for a home program. For high school, a transcript matters for college admission and Hathaway scholarship paperwork rather than for the state. Families on the ESA route have their own notice and assessment items in addition to this.

ESA program (Steamboat Legacy Scholarship)

Wyoming's education savings account: $7,000 a year per student, spent through the Odyssey marketplace. W.S. 21-2-904(f) says an ESA student taught in a non-school setting is not a student of a home-based educational program, and W.S. 21-4-102(d) counts ESA participation as compulsory-attendance compliance. This route adds a yearly notice to the resident district and a yearly assessment. The Wyoming Department of Education sets the application window each year, and the program is still being litigated.

The statute names no date, no form and no method, and the department's ESA page does not mention this notice at all, so the district office is the place to ask what it accepts and when. Assessment · Keep in your files		By the end of the school yearKeep in your files; the Wyoming Department of Education runs the programYour district sets this date, not the state. The ESA parent agreement in W.S. 21-2-904(b)(ii) has a school-age ESA student receive instruction in reading, writing, mathematics, civics including the United States and Wyoming constitutions, history, literature and science, be assessed on academic progress, and take either the statewide assessment under W.S. 21-2-304(a) or a nationally normed achievement exam. The department publishes an ESA Standardized Assessment Options list, which names WY-TOPP along with ITBS, TerraNova, MAP Growth, Stanford 10, CAT, PASS, CLT, ACT and SAT, and says it is not an inclusive list. No statute or department document sets a date, so year end here is only a placeholder; move it to whenever the test chosen is given. What HomeSchool Scribe does in Wyoming

  • Withdrawal from public school: Prepares the notice letter; reminds you 14 days and 2 days before.
  • Course of study for the year: Prepares the course of study; keeps it in records.
  • Nothing to file: keep your own records: Prepares the attendance and hours report and the course of study; keeps it in records.
  • Yearly ESA notice to your school district: Prepares the notice letter; lets you mark it filed with the date.
  • Yearly assessment for each ESA student: Prepares the transcript; keeps it in records.

Attendance, hours, and grades fill in from the lessons you check off, so the documents above come from records you already have. Records are kept for 3 years, the period Wyoming asks for.
