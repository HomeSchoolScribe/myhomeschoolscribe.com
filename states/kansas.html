Homeschool requirements · Kansas

Homeschooling in Kansas

Light paperwork · A notice or an enrollment form, then keep your own records.

Register your homeschool once as a non-accredited private school with the state board and teach for a period substantially equivalent to public schools.

What Kansas asks

NoticeOne-time registration of the school name and address with the Kansas State Department of Education.

Register as a nonaccredited private school
Notice · File online		Once, when you beginKansas State Department of EducationYour district sets this date, not the state. HomeSchool Scribe has deliberately left the reminder off, because any date it picked would be invented. Confirm the date with your district or in your approved plan, then add your own reminder. Kansas does not authorize homeschooling by name in statute. A homeschool counts as a nonaccredited private school, and K.S.A. 72-4346 puts registration of the school's name and address with the State Board of Education on the official custodian of the school's records. KSDE is plain about the shape of it: no fee, no approval process or review, and "Only register your home school once" - there is no annual renewal. The same online form carries an Update option for a change of school name or address and an Inactive option (KSDE's page calls it Non-Active) for when the school closes. It asks for registration type, school name, street address, city, state, county and ZIP. KSDE warns that the form works poorly on a phone and calls a computer or tablet best practice, so this one is worth opening somewhere other than this app. Compulsory attendance in Kansas runs from age 7 to under 18. Neither the statute nor KSDE sets a filing date, so this reminder sits at the start of the school year; a family that starts mid-year registers then instead. That placement is the app's, not a legal deadline.
Tell the previous school the child has transferred
Notice · Mail it		Once, when you beginThe public or accredited school the child attendedOnly for a child leaving a Kansas public or accredited school. KSDE says the school previously attended must be notified by the parent or guardian, and that if this step is not completed the school is required by law to report the student as truant, so this is the step that heads off a truancy referral. It goes to that school, not to the state. KSDE names no form, no method and no date for it, and also says to keep a copy of the homeschool registration for the school the student is withdrawn from. The start-of-year placement here is the app's, not a legal date. A child who has never been enrolled anywhere else has nothing to send.
Keep attendance, hours and a course of study
Records · Keep in your files		By the end of the school yearKeep in your filesNothing is filed after registration: no annual notice, no report, no testing and no evaluation. What Kansas asks instead is that classes run for a period substantially equivalent to public school, which KSDE states as at least 186 days of not less than 6 hours a day, or 1,116 hours a year for grades 1 through 11, taught by competent instructors with no credential required. KSDE gives no hours figure for grade 12. The attendance and hours report is what shows that standard was met. K.S.A. 72-4345 through 72-4347 exist only so that a school a pupil later transfers to can ask for the pupil records, and an accredited school may test a transferring student before accepting credit, so grades and a course of study are worth keeping beside the attendance. A Kansas nonaccredited private school issues its own high school diploma, which KSDE notes the State of Kansas does not recognize as meeting any requirement, and KSDE's fact sheet lists 21 units it recommends for a nonaccredited school's graduates - one more reason to build the transcript as you go. No Kansas statute or KSDE page sets a retention period; three years is this app's planning default.
