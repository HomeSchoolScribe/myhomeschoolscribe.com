Homeschool requirements · California

Homeschooling in California

Moderate paperwork · A notice and a yearly assessment or records check.

Most independent homeschoolers establish a home-based private school by filing the Private School Affidavit each October, keeping an attendance register and course list.

What California asks

NoticePrivate School Affidavit filed online with the state between October 1 and 15 each year (or when the school opens).
Days and hours180 days
SubjectsEnglish, Math, Social Sciences, Science, Fine Arts, Health, Physical Education
AssessmentNone required for private-school-affidavit families.
Records to keepAttendance register, courses of study, and a list of teachers with their qualifications.
Parent qualificationNone under the affidavit option (must be 'capable of teaching').
Worth knowingOther options include a private-school satellite program (PSP), a public charter, or a credentialed tutor.

What HomeSchool Scribe does in California

  • Private School Affidavit: Prepares the course of study; reminds you 14 days and 2 days before; opens the portal and keeps your confirmation.
  • Keep an attendance register and course of study: Prepares the attendance and hours report and the course of study; keeps it in records.

Attendance, hours, and grades fill in from the lessons you check off, so the documents above come from records you already have. Records are kept for 3 years, the period California asks for.
