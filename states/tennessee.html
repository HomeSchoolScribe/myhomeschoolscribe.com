Homeschool requirements · Tennessee

Homeschooling in Tennessee

Moderate paperwork · A notice and a yearly assessment or records check.

Homeschool independently (annual notice, 180 days of 4 hours, attendance, testing in grades 5, 7, and 9) or through a church-related umbrella school with its own requirements.

What Tennessee asks

NoticeAnnual notice of intent to the local director of schools (independent option) or enrollment in a church-related school.
Days and hours180 days, 720 hours a year, 4 hours a day
SubjectsNot specified
AssessmentIndependent option: standardized tests in grades 5, 7, and 9 administered through the district.
Records to keepAttendance records submitted at year end (independent option).
Parent qualificationHigh school diploma or GED (independent option).
Worth knowingMost Tennessee families use the church-related school option, which sets its own reporting rules.

What HomeSchool Scribe does in Tennessee

  • Notice of intent (independent option): Prepares the notice letter; reminds you 14 days and 2 days before; lets you mark it filed with the date.
  • Year-end attendance report: Prepares the attendance and hours report; reminds you 14 days and 2 days before; lets you mark it filed with the date.
  • Standardized test (grades 5, 7, 9): Prepares the transcript; reminds you 14 days and 2 days before.

Attendance, hours, and grades fill in from the lessons you check off, so the documents above come from records you already have. Records are kept for 1 years, the period Tennessee asks for.
