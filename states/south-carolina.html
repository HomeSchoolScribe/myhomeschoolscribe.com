Homeschool requirements · South Carolina

Homeschooling in South Carolina

Moderate paperwork · A notice and a yearly assessment or records check.

Choose one of three options: district approval, SCAIHS membership, or a homeschool association of at least 50 members; all use a 180-day year.

What South Carolina asks

NoticeDepends on option: application to the district (Option 1) or membership in an approved association (Options 2 and 3).
Days and hours180 days, 810 hours a year, 4.5 hours a day
SubjectsReading, Writing, Math, Science, Social Studies, Composition & Literature (grades 7–12)
AssessmentOption 1: a semi-annual progress report submitted to the school district, plus the annual statewide testing and Basic Skills Assessment programs. Options 2 and 3 are exempt from those and follow the standards of their association instead.
Records to keepPlan book or diary, portfolio of samples, and a record of evaluations. The semi-annual progress report goes to the district under Option 1 and is kept by the parent under Option 3.
Parent qualificationHigh school diploma or GED.
Worth knowingOptions 1 and 3 both require 180 days; the four-and-a-half-hour instructional day is Option 1 only.

Deadlines

South Carolina lets a family homeschool more than one way, and the paperwork differs by route. You pick the route once in the app and it tracks only those dates. Districts vary; the app lets you edit any of them.

Option 1: District approval

Apply to your school district each year. 180 days of 4.5 hours, a plan book and portfolio, semi-annual progress reports, and the district's annual testing program.

What HomeSchool Scribe does in South Carolina

  • Application to the district: Prepares the notice letter and the course of study; reminds you 14 days and 2 days before; lets you mark it filed with the date.
  • Semi-annual progress report: Prepares the report card and the attendance and hours report; reminds you 14 days and 2 days before; keeps it in records.
  • Statewide testing program: Prepares the transcript; reminds you 14 days and 2 days before.
  • SCAIHS membership renewal: Prepares the notice letter; reminds you 14 days and 2 days before; opens the portal and keeps your confirmation.
  • Records SCAIHS asks for: Prepares the course of study, the portfolio, the attendance and hours report and the report card; reminds you 14 days and 2 days before; keeps it in records.
  • Accountability association membership: Prepares the notice letter; reminds you 14 days and 2 days before; lets you mark it filed with the date.
  • Plan book and portfolio: Prepares the course of study, the portfolio and the attendance and hours report; reminds you 14 days and 2 days before; keeps it in records.

Attendance, hours, and grades fill in from the lessons you check off, so the documents above come from records you already have. Records are kept for 1 years, the period South Carolina asks for.
