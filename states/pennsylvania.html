Homeschool requirements · Pennsylvania

Homeschooling in Pennsylvania

Paperwork state · Several filings a year with real deadlines.

File a notarized affidavit each year, log 180 days or 900/990 hours, keep a portfolio, test in grades 3, 5, and 8, and have an approved evaluator certify progress annually.

What Pennsylvania asks

NoticeAnnual notarized affidavit with objectives and immunization/health records to the local superintendent by August 1 (or before starting).
Days and hours180 days, 900 hours a year
SubjectsEnglish, Math, Science, Geography, Civics, History of U.S. & PA, Safety & Fire Prevention, Health & Physiology, Physical Education, Music, Art
AssessmentAnnual evaluation by a certified evaluator (certification submitted to the district); standardized tests in grades 3, 5, and 8.
Records to keepPortfolio with a log of reading materials, samples of work, and test results; attendance log.
Parent qualificationHigh school diploma or equivalent.
Worth knowing900 hours for elementary, 990 hours for secondary students.

What HomeSchool Scribe does in Pennsylvania

  • Notarized affidavit with objectives: Prepares the notice letter and the course of study; reminds you 14 days and 2 days before; lets you mark it filed with the date.
  • Portfolio and evaluator certification: Prepares the reading log, the portfolio and the attendance and hours report; reminds you 14 days and 2 days before; lets you mark it filed with the date.
  • Standardized test (grades 3, 5, 8): Prepares the transcript; reminds you 14 days and 2 days before; keeps it in records.

Attendance, hours, and grades fill in from the lessons you check off, so the documents above come from records you already have. Records are kept for 3 years, the period Pennsylvania asks for.
