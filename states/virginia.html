Homeschool requirements · Virginia

Homeschooling in Virginia

Moderate paperwork · A notice and a yearly assessment or records check.

File a notice of intent by August 15 with proof of qualification, then submit evidence of progress by August 1 the following year (or claim the religious exemption).

What Virginia asks

NoticeAnnual notice of intent to the local superintendent by August 15 (or as soon as practicable).
Days and hours180 days
SubjectsNot specified
AssessmentEvidence of progress by August 1: standardized test (composite at or above the 23rd percentile) or an evaluation letter.
Records to keepTest results or evaluation letters.
Parent qualificationHigh school diploma, teaching credential, approved curriculum, or evidence of ability to teach.
Worth knowing180 days is a planning default only.

What HomeSchool Scribe does in Virginia

  • Notice of intent: Prepares the notice letter and the course of study; reminds you 14 days and 2 days before; lets you mark it filed with the date.
  • Evidence of progress: Prepares the report card and the transcript; reminds you 14 days and 2 days before; lets you mark it filed with the date.

Attendance, hours, and grades fill in from the lessons you check off, so the documents above come from records you already have. Records are kept for 2 years, the period Virginia asks for.
