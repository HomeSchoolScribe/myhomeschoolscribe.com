Homeschool requirements · Florida

Homeschooling in Florida

Moderate paperwork · A notice and a yearly assessment or records check.

Send a notice of intent within 30 days of starting, keep a portfolio for two years, and submit an annual evaluation to the district.

What Florida asks

NoticeWritten notice of intent to the county superintendent within 30 days of starting (one time, plus a notice of termination when you stop).
Days and hours180 days
SubjectsNot specified
AssessmentAnnual evaluation submitted to the district: certified-teacher review, standardized test, psychologist evaluation, or other approved method.
Records to keepPortfolio with a log of educational activities and samples of work, kept for two years and available on 15 days' notice.
Parent qualificationNone.
Worth knowing180 days is a planning default only. Florida also allows enrollment in a private 'umbrella' school.

What HomeSchool Scribe does in Florida

  • Notice of intent: Prepares the notice letter; reminds you 14 days and 2 days before; lets you mark it filed with the date.
  • Annual evaluation: Prepares the portfolio, the reading log and the report card; reminds you 14 days and 2 days before; lets you mark it filed with the date.
  • Portfolio (keep 2 years): Prepares the reading log, the portfolio and the attendance and hours report; reminds you 14 days and 2 days before; keeps it in records.

Attendance, hours, and grades fill in from the lessons you check off, so the documents above come from records you already have. Records are kept for 2 years, the period Florida asks for.
