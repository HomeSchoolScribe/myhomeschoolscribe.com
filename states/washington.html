Homeschool requirements · Washington

Homeschooling in Washington

Moderate paperwork · A notice and a yearly assessment or records check.

File a declaration of intent each year, meet a parent qualification, teach the required subjects, and test or assess annually.

What Washington asks

NoticeAnnual declaration of intent to the local superintendent by September 15 or within two weeks of starting.
Days and hours180 days, 1000 hours a year
SubjectsOccupational Education, Science, Math, Language, Social Studies, History, Health, Reading, Writing, Spelling, Art & Music Appreciation
AssessmentAnnual standardized test or assessment by a certified teacher; results kept in the family's records.
Records to keepTest results, immunization records, and academic progress records.
Parent qualification45 college credits, a parent-qualifying course, supervision by a certified teacher, or superintendent approval.
Worth knowingHour totals follow public-school equivalents; 1,000 hours is a common planning target.

What HomeSchool Scribe does in Washington

  • Declaration of intent: Prepares the notice letter; reminds you 14 days and 2 days before; lets you mark it filed with the date.
  • Annual test or assessment: Prepares the report card and the transcript; reminds you 14 days and 2 days before; keeps it in records.

Attendance, hours, and grades fill in from the lessons you check off, so the documents above come from records you already have. Records are kept for 1 years, the period Washington asks for.
