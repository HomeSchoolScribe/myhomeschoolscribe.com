Homeschool requirements · Utah

Homeschooling in Utah

Light paperwork · A notice or an enrollment form, then keep your own records.

File a one-time affidavit or notice with the local district; no testing or reporting afterward.

What Utah asks

NoticeOne-time affidavit/notice to the local school district.
Days and hoursUtah sets no number. HomeSchool Scribe plans against 180 days unless you change it.
SubjectsNot specified
AssessmentNone required.
Records to keepNone required; keep your own.
Parent qualificationNone.
Worth knowing180 days is a planning default only.

One-time notice of intent
Notice · Mail it		Once, when you beginLocal school board of the child's district of residenceFiled once for each child, when the child is withdrawn from school or reaches school age without enrolling, and it does not repeat. A child who starts later files their own first notice, even if an older sibling already filed. Utah Code 53G-6-204(3) sets no deadline, asks for no particular form and no affidavit, and says the notification may take the form of a letter of intent. A parent who already notified the board on or before May 7, 2025, including through the old home-school affidavit, does not file again. Districts differ on where the notice goes and whether they take it by mail, email, or their own web form; the district's own page is where that is listed. The board keeps a record of the notice and acknowledges receipt to the parent within 30 days.
Nothing further to file: keep your own records
Records · Keep in your files		By the end of the school yearKeep in your filesAfter the one-time notice Utah asks for nothing else: no annual report, no testing, and no set number of days or hours. Utah Code 53G-6-204(2)(b) also bars a local school board from requiring records of instruction or attendance, requiring credentials for whoever teaches, inspecting home school facilities, or requiring standardized or other testing. Records kept here are the family's own: attendance, a course of study, and a transcript answer most of what a college or a future school later asks for. The 180 days the app plans against is a planning default, not a Utah rule.

What HomeSchool Scribe does in Utah

  • One-time notice of intent: Prepares the notice letter; reminds you 14 days and 2 days before; lets you mark it filed with the date.
  • Nothing further to file: keep your own records: Prepares the attendance and hours report, the course of study and the transcript; keeps it in records.

Attendance, hours, and grades fill in from the lessons you check off, so the documents above come from records you already have. Records are kept for 1 years, the period Utah asks for.
