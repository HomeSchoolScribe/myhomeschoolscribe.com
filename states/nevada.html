Homeschool requirements · Nevada

Homeschooling in Nevada

Light paperwork · A notice or an enrollment form, then keep your own records.

File a one-time notice of intent with an educational plan; no testing or reporting afterward.

What Nevada asks

NoticeOne-time notice of intent to the local school district (updated only if you move).
Days and hours180 days
SubjectsEnglish, Math, Science, Social Studies
AssessmentNone required.
Records to keepKeep the educational plan; no reporting.
Parent qualificationNone.
Worth knowing180 days is a planning default only.
