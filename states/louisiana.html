Homeschool requirements · Louisiana

Homeschooling in Louisiana

Moderate paperwork · A notice and a yearly assessment or records check.

Apply annually for the state-approved Home Study Program (180 days plus evidence of progress) or register as a non-accredited private school with a simpler notice.

What Louisiana asks

NoticeAnnual application (Home Study Program) or annual notification (registered non-public school) to the Louisiana DOE.
Days and hours180 days
SubjectsNot specified
AssessmentHome Study Program renewals require evidence of progress (test, teacher evaluation, or portfolio); the private-school option does not.
Records to keepKeep a curriculum outline, samples, and attendance.
Parent qualificationNone.

Deadlines

Louisiana lets a family homeschool more than one way, and the paperwork differs by route. You pick the route once in the app and it tracks only those dates. Districts vary; the app lets you edit any of them.

Home Study Program

Apply to the state within 15 days of starting, then renew every year with evidence of progress: a standardized test, a teacher's evaluation, or a portfolio. 180 days.

Registered nonpublic school

Register with the state within 30 days of starting and every year after. 180 days, attendance kept, no testing. The simpler route.

What HomeSchool Scribe does in Louisiana

  • Home Study Program application: Prepares the notice letter and the course of study; reminds you 14 days and 2 days before; opens the portal and keeps your confirmation.
  • Home Study Program renewal: Prepares the notice letter, the course of study and the report card; reminds you 14 days and 2 days before; opens the portal and keeps your confirmation.
  • Evidence of progress: Prepares the transcript, the portfolio and the report card; reminds you 14 days and 2 days before; keeps it in records.
  • Attendance record: Prepares the attendance and hours report; reminds you 14 days and 2 days before; keeps it in records.
  • Nonpublic school registration: Prepares the notice letter; reminds you 14 days and 2 days before; opens the portal and keeps your confirmation.

Attendance, hours, and grades fill in from the lessons you check off, so the documents above come from records you already have. Records are kept for 3 years, the period Louisiana asks for.
